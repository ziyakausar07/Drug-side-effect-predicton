Clinical Risk Stratification & Monitoring Reference
Low Risk Standard Care

Biomarkers and clinical parameters remain within standard physiological limits. Standard dosage titration and routine clinical reviews are typically indicated.

Moderate Risk Enhanced Surveillance

Borderline vitals, renal/hepatic strain, or potential drug-drug interactions detected. Prudent dosage scheduling and close therapeutic monitoring recommended.

High Risk Critical Precaution

Heightened likelihood of adverse drug reaction (ADR) due to prior reaction history, organ impairment, or critical contraindications. Immediate clinician review advised.
